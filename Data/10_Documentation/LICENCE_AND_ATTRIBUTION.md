* **Dataset:** Dataset B (Elevation Observations & Road Gradients)
* **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
* **Originating Agency:** European Space Agency (ESA) & Airbus Defence and Space
* **Attribution Notice:**
  > *"Copernicus Climate Change Service (C3S) / European Space Agency (ESA). Copernicus Digital Elevation Model (DEM) GLO-90, accessed via Open-Meteo elevation service. The data is provided under the Copernicus Open Access Policy."*
* **Spatial Specifications:** 90-meter global grid (3 arc-seconds), WGS84 horizontal datum, EGM96 vertical geoid datum.
* **Website:** https://spacedata.copernicus.eu/collections/copernicus-digital-elevation-model
